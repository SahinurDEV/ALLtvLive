## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
ALLtvLive is a clean, fast way to browse and watch publicly available live TV
streams from around the world, aggregated from the open-source iptv-org project.

WHAT YOU GET
• Thousands of live channels organised by country, category and language
• A distraction-free live-TV player — tap to reveal controls, clean while playing
• Fullscreen and picture-in-picture playback
• Volume control and one-tap mute
• Save your favourite channels for quick access
• Continue where you left off with recently-watched
• Add your own custom stream URLs (HLS / MP4)
• Dark, modern interface built for the living room and the phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account required
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers
• Your favourites, history and settings stay on your device

ABOUT THE CONTENT
ALLtvLive does not host, upload or redistribute any video. It is a directory of
publicly available streams that are delivered directly by their original
broadcasters. If you believe a stream infringes your rights, contact us from the
in-app About screen and we will remove it promptly.

Enjoy live TV, everywhere.
```
